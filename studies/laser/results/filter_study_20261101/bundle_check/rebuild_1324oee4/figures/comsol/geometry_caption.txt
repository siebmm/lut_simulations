Specimen geometry and laser-ultrasonic acquisition layout for (a) the full weld and (b) the partial weld, with (c) an enlarged view of the partial-weld floor, J-bevel and lack of fusion (LOF). The specimens are 150 mm wide and 25.4 mm thick; hatched regions denote 15 mm absorbing strips. The partial weld has 9 mm fill, placing its exposed floor 16.4 mm below the original outer surface (OD), from which all depths are measured. Orange triangles mark the shoulder sources S0/S4 and central sources S1-S3. Blue and green markers denote plate and central receivers, respectively (337 total for the full weld; 331 for the partial weld). Black dashed rectangles show the SAFT grids: x = -6 to 6 mm, depth = 5-25.25 mm for the full weld; x = -7.5 to 7.5 mm, depth = 16.65-25.25 mm for the partial weld. The red dashed line and open circles mark the prescribed LOF and its tips at 18.467-20.967 mm depth. Grey internal boundaries are bonded except along the LOF; the dotted root centreline denotes a bonded domain partition.
